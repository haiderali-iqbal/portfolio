## 📊 Featured Project Spotlight

### **Biological Sex Differences and the Limits of Human Endurance**
*A Bioinformatics Approach to Ultramarathon Data*

- **Big Data Scale:** Analysed a global dataset of **7.4 million race records** integrated with National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) lipid profile benchmarks.
- **Methodology:** Developed reproducible data pipelines in **R (v4.3.1)** using `tidyverse`, `dplyr`, and `rstatix`. Performed Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Welch's t-tests.
- **Key Finding:** Demonstrated a non-linear performance convergence and reversal over 200 km distances, associated with distinct female lipid oxidation efficiency and pacing resilience.
